1.7.1 - Auto Pilot

Auto Join actually does its job now, plus sound stickers and a bit of polish.
  • Auto Join is now server-assisted. Your saved look (color/skin, hats and tag) is applied before the join animation plays, so you pop in already dressed instead of morphing into it a second after joining. Your rank still has the final say on what sticks.
    • The Auto Join suggestions now cover everything you can actually wear: normal colors, the freepope (dunce) cap, blessed skins, pope skins, and normal / blessed / mod / unlocked vault hats.
  • Added sound stickers — stickers that play a sound effect. The bubble shows the sticker and then closes when the clip finishes. Currently /sticker car and /sticker spaghetti (pope-only).
  • Sticker images now scale to fit inside the speech bubble instead of spilling out of it.
  • Added a "Disable DMs" toggle in /settings (General tab). Turn it on and nobody can open a Direct Message with you.
  • Added /banish for popes. Run it in any room and everyone in every other room gets yanked into the one you're in.
  • The goldenhat vault hat is finally unlockable — it was listed but had no unlock code before.
